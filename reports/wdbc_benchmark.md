# Appendix: WDBC benchmark

Generated by `experiments/wdbc_benchmark.py`. **Not used by the app.**

Wisconsin Diagnostic Breast Cancer (`sklearn.datasets.load_breast_cancer`): 569 cases, 30 features, 212 malignant. Stratified 10-fold CV over all cases; mean ± SD across folds. Recall uses the default 0.5 threshold.

| Model | AUROC | Accuracy | Recall |
|---|---|---|---|
| Logistic regression | 0.9947 ± 0.0065 | 97.54% ± 1.95% | 95.26% ± 5.22% |
| SVM (RBF) | 0.9956 ± 0.0055 | 97.54% ± 1.95% | 96.21% ± 3.56% |
| Random forest | 0.9902 ± 0.0133 | 95.61% ± 3.06% | 93.35% ± 7.13% |
| LightGBM | 0.9926 ± 0.0085 | 96.67% ± 2.54% | 93.35% ± 5.72% |
| XGBoost | 0.9927 ± 0.0098 | 96.67% ± 2.98% | 94.78% ± 6.55% |

Every model lands near AUROC 0.99, so WDBC does not discriminate between methods; that is why the main project focuses on the harder mammographic task.
